Welcome

Hello, I'm Jo.

A midwife by trade, but also a keen pottery enthusiast. I've spent ten years making wonderful pottery, teaching workshops and firing my kiln. I specialise in bespoke baby prints onto ceramic, and raised 3D casts. It's lovely to finally have a place to call home in Westbourne.

Wessex FM Best New Business Award winner 2018.

More about the studio

Bespoke baby prints · The gift no one else makes

And for the smallest hands in your life.

Bring me your baby or child and let's capture beautiful prints to treasure forever. A gentle press into soft clay, capturing every tiny detail, hand finished and personalised, on ceramic or as a raised 3D cast. Ready in about three weeks, with framing options in the studio and a postal service if you need it.

Around the kiln
