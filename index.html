Welcome

Hello, I'm Jo.

I've spent ten years making, teaching and firing, and now my wheels and my kiln have a home in Westbourne. Come in for an hour or an evening, sit down at the wheel, and see what your hands can do. Never touched clay before? Most people haven't. That's the fun part.
